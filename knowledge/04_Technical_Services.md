# 04. Technical Services & Commercial Positioning

## Free Product vs. Chargeable Services
- **No Software Licence Fee:** TBConnect and SmartPatch are offered without recurring software license fees. Researchers can adopt the core mobile software platform for clinical trials without software seat licensing.
- **Chargeable Technical Services:** Paid services are available for study-specific requirements, including:
  1. Dedicated study hosting and tenant infrastructure.
  2. Electronic Data Capture (EDC) API integrations (REDCap, OpenClinica, proprietary institutional databases).
  3. Custom workflow configuration and protocol localization (language, study-specific intake variables).
  4. Ongoing protocol maintenance and technical support packages.
  5. Physical TB SmartPatch consumable units (dispenser boxes).

## Commercial Boundaries & Rules for AI SDR
1. **Never imply that all services are free.** Clarify that study-specific hosting, custom integrations, and specialized technical support are chargeable services.
2. **Do not provide pricing quotes or cost estimates.** All pricing and commercial proposals depend on study scale, site count, hosting needs, and integration complexity, and must be reviewed and approved by an authorized human team member.
3. **Primary Call to Action:** Offer to coordinate a 15-minute protocol feasibility discussion with the technical team or arrange a sample evaluation patch kit for lab review.
