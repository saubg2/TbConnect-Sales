# 03. Research Use Cases & Supported Clinical Scenarios

## Supported Study Designs
1. **TB Vaccine & Preventive Treatment Trials:** Standardizing induration cutoffs across multi-center cohorts to ensure regulatory defensibility and publication-grade data.
2. **Active Case Finding (ACF) & Decentralized Screening:** Empowering community health workers during home visits to screen household contacts without requiring participants to travel to distant health facilities.
3. **Cy-TB & SIILTIBCY Implementation Studies:** Validating next-generation recombinant ESAT6/CFP10 skin test delivery in primary healthcare networks.
4. **Pediatric TB Infection Cohorts:** Minimizing missed reads and lost-to-follow-up in children through automated reminders and home-based optical verification.

## Active & Completed Evidence Proof Points
- **ICMR-NIIRNCD Jodhpur Household Screening Study (India):**
  Active 2-year field research project screening ~2,400 household contacts across rural and peri-urban Rajasthan. Uses Cy-TB + SmartPatch + TBConnect Android platform for decentralized community health worker visits. Approved by ICMR Headquarters.
- **SCAF-TB Tajikistan (KNCV Tuberculosis Foundation):**
  Multi-country pediatric and household contact investigation in high-burden Central Asia. Expanding access to child TB preventive treatment by removing clinic return barriers.
- **SCAF-TB Tanzania (KNCV Tuberculosis Foundation):**
  Operational research cohort across Sub-Saharan Africa evaluating digital Cy-TB delivery in primary health facilities.
- **Pune Specialist Concordance Trial (Completed):**
  Demonstrated 100% agreement in diagnostic classification between TBConnect AI reading and specialist PhD expert readers.
- **Pune Behavioral Usability Study (Completed):**
  100% patch retention across the complete 48–72h window with 0% moderate-to-severe dermatological reactions and zero disruption to daily agricultural or household routines.
