# 02. TB SmartPatch Technical Specifications & Usage

## Physical & Mechanical Details
- **Reading Aperture:** Central 40mm circular opening with protective peel-back SmartCut™ flap. Healthcare workers perform the Mantoux intradermal wheal injection through the aperture without removing the patch.
- **Optical Crosshairs:** 4 cardinal millimeter reference ticks provide sub-millimeter geometric scale and enable tilt/curvature mathematical correction.
- **Multi-Spectral Targets:** 4 calibrated color reference dots (coral, gold, blue, green) dynamically normalize ambient color temperature, sunlight shifts, and skin pigmentation across Fitzpatrick phototypes I–VI.
- **Digital Binding:** Serialized matrix 2D QR code permanently binds the physical patch to the participant ID.
- **Skin Adhesion & Biocompatibility:** Medical-grade hypoallergenic polyacrylate adhesive, latex-free, ISO 10993 biocompatibility certified. Validated for 48–72 hours of continuous wear without skin irritation.
- **Packaging:** Compact single-use dispenser boxes of 50 or 100 units. Ambient storage (15°C–30°C); 24-month validated shelf life.

## Why Plastic Rulers Fail
Conventional ruler/ballpoint methods suffer from 25–40% inter-reader variance, pronounced digit preference (clustering at 5mm, 10mm, 15mm), 3D parallax distortion on curved arms, and erythema confusion. SmartPatch provides an on-skin millimeter scale that mathematically eliminates tactile and visual subjectivity.

## Usage Protocol
1. Clean forearm according to standard clinical trial protocol.
2. Peel backing and apply SmartPatch comfortably on the volar aspect of the forearm.
3. Open SmartCut™ protective flap, perform intradermal injection (0.1 mL antigen) using standard tuberculin syringe.
4. Smooth down protective flap. Leave patch in place until reading visit at 48–72 hours.
